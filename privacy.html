Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Clipveil is built so that your screenshots stay on your Mac. This policy explains the very small amount of data involved.

What stays on your Mac

Screenshots, recordings, OCR text, capture history and settings are created and stored only on your device. Clipveil has no account system, no analytics and no cloud upload.

What is sent over the network

The Mac App Store version asks Apple whether your Apple ID has bought Clipveil Pro or started the trial. This goes through Apple’s App Store service on your Mac; Clipveil sends nothing of its own.

The direct-download version also checks clipveil.app/appcast.xml for updates. This is a plain download of the update list; no personal data or usage statistics are sent.

Purchases

Clipveil Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Website

This website does not use cookies or tracking scripts.

Your rights

We hold no order or account information about you. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that correspondence by writing to support@clipveil.app.
