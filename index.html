Faces are found, never hidden by surprise

Every face Clipveil finds gets an orange outline — it never hides anyone without asking. Click the ones that shouldn't be shared, or hit Black out all. Missed one? Draw a mosaic over it in a second. Prefer hands-off? Automatic mode covers faces as you capture.

Copy the words, not the pixels

Double-click any line of text in a screenshot and press ⌘C. Line breaks and columns are kept, so tables paste straight into a spreadsheet. ⌃⇧4 grabs text from anywhere on screen — no screenshot needed. Recognition runs on your Mac.

Simple yet powerful editor

Everything you need to explain a screenshot, nothing you have to learn.

  • Arrows — straight, bold, curved or double-headed; dashed if you like.
  • Rotate anything — grab the knob above a box, label or mosaic and turn it; it snaps to 45°.
  • Numbered steps — 1, 2, 3… or A, B, C… that renumber themselves.
  • Highlight & spotlight — mark a line, or dim everything except the part that matters.
  • Shapes & text — boxes and ovals with outline, translucent or solid fill; text in five fonts with an outline or label style.
  • One key per tool — A for Arrow, T for Text, B for Black out, N for Number. ⌘Z undoes everything, crop included.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Download, grant Screen Recording permission, and start capturing. A license key is only needed for Pro.

Does Clipveil upload my screenshots?

Never. Capturing, OCR and editing all happen on your Mac. The only network request is a license check for Pro.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Clipveil keeps working as the free version. Pro features show an upgrade prompt.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — full refund within 14 days, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

How many Macs can I use?

A Pro license activates on 2 Macs. You can move it by deactivating from the License window.
