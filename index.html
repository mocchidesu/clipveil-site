FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Download, grant Screen Recording permission, and start capturing. A license key is only needed for Pro.

Does Clipveil upload my screenshots?

Never. Capturing, OCR and editing all happen on your Mac. The only network request is a license check for Pro.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Clipveil keeps working as the free version. Pro features show an upgrade prompt.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — full refund within 14 days, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

How many Macs can I use?

A Pro license activates on 2 Macs. You can move it by deactivating from the License window.
