Last updated: November 16, 2026

Clipveil Pro is bought through the Mac App Store, so Apple takes the payment and issues refunds. To ask for one, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and choose “Request a refund” for Clipveil Pro.
